## TOP 10 HIGHEST-RISK GAPS

1. **PSP amplitude is unpublished:** strict `response_scale=None` silently normalizes the kernel peak to 1, equivalent to choosing `V0 about 1.87` for the current taus.
2. **`tau_m=.10` and `tau_s=.02` are local:** neither value appears in Eq. (1) or Table I, yet they set temporal summation and PSP peak delay.
3. **`V_inh(t)` equivalence is unproven:** strict lacks a complete continuous trajectory, while the public paper also omits its numerical form.
4. **Winner equivalence is unproven:** strict earliest firing is not demonstrably identical to the paper's largest soma membrane-potential winner.
5. **`timing_tolerance=.03` is a high-impact local semantic:** it controls prediction confirmation, burst classification, Scenario matching, and contributor credit.
6. **Scenario 1 attribution is a local operationalization:** arrival-window contribution can weaken genuinely causal continuous-PSP synapses.
7. **Synaptic delay mapping is incomplete:** strict current-delay is not demonstrably identical to the paper's printed delay rule and does not compensate PSP peak latency.
8. **Passenger SSTD geometry is underdetermined:** `[0,40000]`, endpoint centers, clipping, and `sigma=spacing` are local choices.
9. **Passenger decoding is entirely local:** half-spacing search, overlap ordering, and tie averaging are not specified by Zhang et al.
10. **Fig.9 evaluation windows are not paper-reconstructible:** warmup 5,904 and rolling window 400 are unpublished; the ratio-of-sums metric itself is reference-supported but must not be confused with pointwise MAPE.

## Required Conclusions

1. **Does `kernel_scale` still exist?** Yes. It is active in `DSDynamicsParams.kernel_scale` and multiplies every distal PSP.
2. **Is it equivalent to an unpublished `V0` choice?** Yes. Under strict `response_scale=None`, it chooses `V0=1/unscaled_peak`; with current taus this is approximately 1.87, making the PSP peak exactly 1.
3. **Does current kernel peak normalization have paper support?** No explicit support was found. Eq. (1) exposes `V0`, and Table I does not prescribe unit-peak normalization.
4. **Do `tau_m/tau_s` have paper numerical support?** No. The paper names them as layer-related shape parameters but publishes no numerical values in the equation, prose, or Table I.
5. **Does `timing_tolerance=.03` have paper support?** No numerical basis was found. It is a local operationalization of approximate/same-time matching.
6. **Is `V_inh` fully implemented?** No. Strict does not simulate a complete continuous trajectory; because the public paper omits its numerical form, exact author-code equivalence remains unknown rather than proven mismatched.
7. **Is the soma winner strictly equivalent to the paper?** Equivalence is unproven. Strict selects earliest predicted firing, while the paper describes the largest soma membrane potential followed by inhibition; current evidence does not establish identity or inevitable failure.
8. **Three highest-value next experiments:** (a) a preregistered `V0 x tau_m x tau_s` sensitivity grid with kernel normalization disabled as a separate diagnostic; (b) implement a read-only/full Eq. (3) candidate replay comparing explicit `V_inh(t)` and largest-soma winner against earliest firing; (c) jointly audit `timing_tolerance` and peak-aligned delay using column-correct/time-wrong, burst, Scenario-1 attribution, and five-step rollout metrics. None should replace strict defaults until independently justified.
